Blog settings

These control the name, tagline, description and footer shown on the landing page, the posts list and around every post.

You need to be logged in to change settings. Go to the dashboard to log in, then come back here.

Your own title for your pages — the brand on your posts and list pages, the feed title, and the tab title. Each account has its own; a fresh one starts as the account name. The landing page shows the first account's title.

Shown at the bottom of your pages. When the credit below is on and this text does not already mention postwisp, "· Powered by postwisp" is appended to it.

Off removes the credit from your pages entirely (footer text, if any, still shows).

The default language for visitors who have not picked one. A visitor's own choice (the switcher in the site header) and their browser's Accept-Language header both override it. Labels are each language's native name.

Stored on the server with your other settings, so every browser you log in from shares it. Without a key, the Unsplash button in the editor asks for one when first used.
